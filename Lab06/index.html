Tourist Places

Image Name Description
Eiffel Tower An iron tower in Paris, France, built in 1889. It is one of the most visited places in the world.
Taj Mahal A white marble tomb in Agra, India, built by Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.
Great Wall of China A very long wall across northern China that was built to protect the country from invaders.
Statue of Liberty A large copper statue in New York, USA. It was a gift from France and is a symbol of freedom.
