Tourist Places of Pakistan

1. Faisal Mosque

Faisal Mosque is located in Islamabad. It is one of the largest mosques in Pakistan and is famous for its unique design and beautiful surroundings.

Learn more about Faisal Mosque

2. Hunza Valley

Hunza Valley is located in Gilgit-Baltistan. It is famous for its high mountains, green valleys, clear rivers, and beautiful natural views.

Learn more about Hunza Valley

3. Badshahi Mosque

Badshahi Mosque is located in Lahore. It was built during the Mughal period and is known for its impressive architecture and historical importance.

Learn more about Badshahi Mosque

4. Skardu

Skardu is a beautiful destination in Gilgit-Baltistan. It is surrounded by mountains, lakes, and valleys and serves as a starting point for many mountain expeditions.

Learn more about Skardu

5. Mohenjo-daro

Mohenjo-daro is an ancient archaeological site in Sindh. It was an important city of the Indus Valley Civilization and shows the advanced planning of early cities.

Learn more about Mohenjo-daro

6. Neelum Valley

Neelum Valley is located in Azad Jammu and Kashmir. It is popular for its green forests, flowing rivers, mountain views, and peaceful atmosphere.

Learn more about Neelum Valley

7. Minar-e-Pakistan

Minar-e-Pakistan is a famous monument in Lahore. It stands at the place where the Lahore Resolution was passed in 1940 and represents the history of Pakistan.

Learn more about Minar-e-Pakistan

8. Saif-ul-Malook Lake

Saif-ul-Malook Lake is located near Naran in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It is famous for its clear water, surrounding mountains, and breathtaking scenery.

Learn more about Saif-ul-Malook Lake

9. Makli Necropolis

Makli Necropolis is located near Thatta in Sindh. It is a historic cemetery containing thousands of tombs and monuments with beautiful stone carvings.

Learn more about Makli Necropolis

10. Naltar Valley

Naltar Valley is located near Gilgit. It is known for its forests, colorful lakes, snowy mountains, and opportunities for skiing and outdoor activities.

Learn more about Naltar Valley
